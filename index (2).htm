Course Information

Gain life-saving skills for seizure first aid and 4everyday epilepsy safety in this free online course.

Epilepsy affects millions worldwide, and understanding it is the first step to safety and support. This program explains the basics, including what epilepsy is, its causes, and the different types of seizures. You will learn to recognize triggers and early warning signs. The myths that often surround the condition are also introduced. Diagnosis, treatment options, and daily living with epilepsy are also covered, providing clear knowledge for both personal awareness and caregiving situations.

Seizure management is explored in depth, along with practical first aid guidance. Topics include recognizing seizure patterns, protecting someone during an episode, managing breathing and positioning, and knowing when to call for help. Everyday safety is emphasized, from home and school to sports, travel, and workplace settings. The course also highlights the role of family, friends, and technology, such as alert devices, helping create a safer and more supportive environment.

By completing this course, you gain skills that can save lives and reduce risks. You will learn how to provide immediate support, recognize emergencies, and develop action plans for various scenarios. Whether for personal confidence, professional caregiving, or community awareness, the knowledge gained offers lasting value. Start learning today to make a difference in the life of someone with epilepsy.
